About

Building the coalitions cancer patients depend on.

Elo Mapelu is a public health advocate and civil society leader based in Nairobi. He holds a degree in Public Policy and Administration from Kenyatta University and a diploma in Pharmacy, a foundation that shapes his work at the intersection of patient care and health policy.

He is a founding member and current Chairman of Henzo Kenya, a community-based organization supporting people living with leukemia, GIST, and other rare cancers, and Chairman of the Kenyan Network of Cancer Organizations (KENCO) — the national umbrella body coordinating Kenya's civil society response to cancer.
